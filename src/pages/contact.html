FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Phoenix Industrial, Durban, and run door-to-door on all the major national routes in South Africa. Tell us the pick-up and drop-off towns and we'll confirm the route.

Is my car insured while it's on the truck?

Yes. All our trucks and trailers carry full comprehensive insurance and goods-in-transit (GIT) cover.

Can I find out where my car is?

Every truck has a 24-hour satellite tracking system. Ask our team for an update at any time during the trip.

Do you only work with dealerships?

No. We move cars for dealer groups, private owners and corporate fleet owners, from a single car to a full load.

Do you move anything other than cars?

No. M.I.E. Logistics only moves cars and other passenger vehicles like SUVs and bakkies, on dedicated car carriers.

How do I prepare my car?

Remove personal belongings and loose items, note or photograph any existing marks, and leave the keys and any alarm or immobiliser instructions with the driver at collection.
