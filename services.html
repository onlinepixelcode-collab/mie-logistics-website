Services

What wemove.

Only cars, only on car carriers. Four ways we move them, door to door, on every major route in South Africa.

01

Dealer stock transfers

New and pre-owned stock moved between branches, groups and regions. We've moved cars for Bidvest McCarthy Nissan, SMG BMW, Supertech BMW and JLR.

  • Branch-to-branch and inter-group moves
  • Single cars or full loads
  • Collection at the dealership, delivery to the dealership
02

Prestige & supercars

Supercars and collector cars driven on carefully, strapped down on the deck and watched by satellite until they're handed over.

  • Low-clearance cars handled with care
  • Comprehensive and goods-in-transit cover
  • 24-hour satellite tracking on every truck
03

Private cars

Relocating, buying from another province or sending a car to family. We collect it at your door and deliver it to theirs.

  • Door-to-door, no depot drop-off
  • Quotes by phone, WhatsApp or online
  • Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00, Sat 08:00–13:00
04

Corporate fleets

Fleet roll-outs, returns and relocations for business and corporate fleet owners, from a few cars to a full load on one truck.

  • Planned around your fleet schedule
  • National routes from Durban
  • One point of contact for the whole move
