About M.I.E.

19 yearson the road.

A Durban car carrier company that has moved cars since September 2007. The promise hasn't changed since the first load: your vehicle, our priority.

From cross-border
to every national route.

We started in September 2007 moving cars across borders. In 2015 we brought everything home and focused on South Africa's own national routes.

Today dealer groups, private owners and corporate fleet managers trust us with their cars, on our own trucks, with 24-hour satellite tracking and full comprehensive and GIT cover on every truck and trailer.
